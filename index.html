Who We Are

A Laboratory Solutions Company,
Built on Technical Expertise

SRF Corporation is a laboratory solutions company serving the pharmaceutical, life science, diagnostic and research sectors of Bangladesh. We supply Microbiology, QC, R&D, Biotech and Molecular laboratory products, along with engineering equipment and technical support.

Our team combines deep technical expertise and industry experience to deliver reliable scientific and engineering solutions — with a clear understanding of the technical, operational and regulatory requirements of modern laboratories.

We are trusted by leading pharmaceutical manufacturers, universities, research institutes and hospitals across the country, and aim to be a long-term partner delivering quality products, application support and reliable after-sales service.

  • Products for QC, Microbiology, R&D, Biotech & Molecular labs
  • Engineering equipment, power & genuine parts
  • Technical, operational & regulatory understanding
  • Application support & after-sales service
